# Study 8 — investigator fix, pre-registered protocol (Access-2026-46118 revision)

Committed before any configuration is run on the cases below. The fixes were designed after Study 7
(`external_validation/expert_study`) showed that the frozen Hybrid's trigger fires on unflagged evidence gaps but its
investigator does not request the evidence. Study 7's cases are therefore development data for the fixes; this study's
36 cases are the confirmatory test.

## Fixes under test (`agentic_experiments/hybrid_v2.py`; frozen `hybrid.py` unchanged)
- **HY2-F** — same trigger; when it fires, a rule calls every available approved read-only tool once; deterministic
  governance decides. No LLM in the investigation.
- **HY2-L** — same trigger; when it fires, all four domain agents run (reasoning effort "medium"), each still seeing only
  its own domain's evidence but allowed to request any available approved tool, with a three-line addendum on stale and
  absent evidence; every requested tool is executed; the requesting agent is re-run on the results; the coordinator
  proposes; deterministic governance decides.

## Cases (`cases_fx_v1.yaml`, generated by `make_cases.py`, seed 20261023)
- 20 unflagged-gap decisive cases from a fixed template grid (5 templates x absent/cross-domain/stale = 15; 3 resolved-
  alarm templates x stale = 3; 2 grid cells drawn again at random), 4 controls, and 12 standard cases (3 per stratum).
- Author admissible actions are in `author_labels.yaml`, sealed in this commit; this is the primary key.

## Arms
DET, AF-M (fetch fields flagged missing), AF-MS (also stale fields), FETCH-ALL (all available tools), HY2-F,
HY (frozen Hybrid, reasoning none, 3 runs), HY2-L (3 runs), B6 (enhanced Agentic-only, reasoning medium + governance
rules, 1 run). Development check: HY2-F and HY2-L (1 run) on the 96 earlier cases (original 48 + Study 7's 48), scored on
the author keys and reported separately as non-confirmatory.

Budget caps: 2,200 LLM calls and 1.6M tokens; probe call first; stop on any account error.

## Pre-specified analyses (fresh cases, author key, majority of runs)
- **Primary:** on the 20 unflagged-gap decisive cases, HY2-L vs HY and HY2-F vs HY, paired exact McNemar,
  Holm-corrected over the two.
- **Secondary:** HY2-L vs FETCH-ALL, HY2-L vs B6, HY2-F vs FETCH-ALL (accuracy and tool calls per case); LLM calls and
  tokens per case; HY2-L stability across 3 runs.
- **No regression:** on the 12 standard cases and 4 controls, HY2-L and HY2-F vs HY (counts and exact McNemar).
- Results are reported whatever they show, including a fix that does not improve on HY or on FETCH-ALL.
