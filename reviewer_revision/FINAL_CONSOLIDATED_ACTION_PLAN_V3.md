## Objective
Determine whether selective agentic evidence investigation adds measurable policy-grounded governance action agreement beyond strong deterministic retrieval, at what cost, latency, risk and containment. Report positive, null and negative outcomes. Synthetic-policy performance is not validated PM preference or real-world effectiveness.

## W1 — Protocol and independent oracle freeze (week 1)
- Author a **reference governance policy specification** independently of AAF implementation and all B1/B2/B4 outputs. Trace each rule to Google SRE, DORA, NIST SSDF, FinOps or other specific external guidance **where applicable**, and separately disclose operational assumptions that those sources do not prescribe. External guidance does not itself uniquely determine actions. Record action vocabulary, evidence sufficiency, admissible sets, rationale, indeterminate-case/exclusion rules, rule conflicts and provenance. Separately implement and test policy-to-answer consistency; code checks are not external validity.
- Freeze AAF/B2 SHAs, B2 policy/trigger-derived tool mapping independent of B4 outcomes, shared z(I), evidence, tools, budgets, exact models/prompts/seeds, run logging, scripts and software versions.
- Preferred independent families C30/A60/I60/M30=180; contingency C20/A40/I40/M20=120. Primary A+I n=120 or 80. One case per genuinely independent family. Predeclare feasibility rule for contingency, precision analysis under 10–20% discordance, and report likely Outcome C at n=80. I is exactly half mapped-null and half nontrivial evidence selection.
- Primary B4 minus B2 A+I case-level admissible-action agreement. Stochastic B3/B4/B5/B6 (and B7 if used) correct if >=3/5 runs admissible. B1/B2 scored once, identical-input determinism check. Tango paired-score 95% CI via R PropCIs::scoreci.mp; exact McNemar via R exact2x2. Verify function signatures and lock versions.
- Secondary family (Holm adjusted): B4-B3 **case-level mean total tokens** over C+A+I+M, B4-B3 **case-level mean end-to-end wall-clock latency** over C+A+I+M, B4-B6 case-level action agreement **A+I**. For tokens and latency: case-level paired percentile bootstrap CI with 10,000 resamples, preserving zero differences; two-sided paired sign-flip permutation p-value on case-level mean differences, preserving zero differences. Predefine number of permutations/exact enumeration threshold, RNG seed, handling of missing/failed runs, and whether confidence intervals are nominal or multiplicity-adjusted. B4-B6 Tango CI and exact McNemar p-value. Holm adjust three secondary p-values.
- SESOI +/-10pp (~one additional correct decision in ten uncertain cases), an operational assumption, not elicited PM preference. A: d>=+10pp and CI L>0; B: 0<d<+10pp and L>0; C: otherwise; D: d<=-10pp and CI U<0; E: -10pp<d<0 and U<0. Outcome A/B does not prove true effect >=10pp. B4-B6 uses same A-E; only A/B supports **narrow superior agreement** claim, not unqualified architectural superiority. If B7 included, apply analogous qualification.
- **Week-1 auditor recruitment is an active task:** identify and approach qualified independent PM/Delivery and SRE/DevOps practitioners, secure availability for a 40-case blind audit, and confirm consent and conflicts before scenario authoring. Check exact six reviewer comments, IEEE decision category, and whether human validation is explicitly requested.
- Publish immutable public tag or OSF preregistration BEFORE any confirmatory scenario authoring; preregister independent audit optional-contingency treatment and sensitivity analysis.

## W2 — Independent benchmark and bounded practitioner audit (weeks 2-3)
- Construct policy-grounded, realistic synthetic cases without running B4 or inspecting confirmatory outcomes; record family provenance, scenario, evidence, policy rationale, admissible set, ambiguity, source mapping and indeterminate/exclusion status. Old 48 cases remain exploratory. Benchmark and answer key freeze before system runs.
- **Audit:** prospective stratified random 40 cases (10 per C/A/I/M; I subgroup split 5+5). Two qualified external practitioners, PM/Delivery and SRE/DevOps, independent of code, benchmark and authors' reference answers, **not briefed on the study hypotheses or B4 design**, label primary action and admissible sets blind to oracle and system outputs. Consent, COI and optional compensation/acknowledgment. Compute between-rater Cohen kappa, oracle-to-rater agreement, Jaccard for admissible sets, agreement rates and disagreement taxonomy.
- Practitioner disagreement **is not automatically oracle error**. Retain all frozen cases in primary analysis. Report prespecified sensitivity excluding *substantively disputed* audited cases only as secondary analysis, with clear limits (audited subset vs full benchmark). Do not silently revise labels or redefine primary sample. If audit unavailable despite documented week-1 recruitment attempts, document missing validation; only omit it under prospectively registered feasibility contingency and do not claim external practitioner validation. **Scientific cost:** the R4/R5 criticism that authors constructed the answer key remains materially unresolved; code-level policy checks and external citations do not establish independent oracle validity. - Audit is **not** PM usability or real-world effectiveness validation.
