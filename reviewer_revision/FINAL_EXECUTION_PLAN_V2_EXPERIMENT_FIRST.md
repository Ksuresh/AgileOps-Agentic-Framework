## Objective and limitation
Evaluate selective Agentic AI versus strong deterministic retrieval and agentic baselines with transparent, reproducible, policy-grounded synthetic experiments. Policy-oracle results establish agreement with documented governance specifications, NOT independent practitioner validity, PM acceptance, or real-world decision superiority.

## P1 (days 1-4 tentative): preregistration and anti-circular oracle
- Construct a versioned **independent reference policy specification** from explicit governance requirements, evidence sufficiency, safety constraints and admissible actions, not by reusing AAF implementation or B1/B2/B4 outputs. Record authorship/provenance, separate reference implementation and tests. Independent code-level consistency checks verify policy-to-oracle mappings; they do not independently validate real-world policy realism.
- Predeclare indeterminate cases, admissible action sets, scenario exclusion rules and reference conflicts. Scenario authors cannot see confirmatory model results or tune against them. Freeze scenario templates, family independence, generation, answer assignment, and data provenance.
- Freeze AAF/B2 hashes, same z(I) trigger, tool/evidence access and budgets, model versions, prompts, temperatures, tool schemas, seeds, budgets, run logs.
- Preferred independent cases C30/A60/I60/M30, total 180, primary A+I n=120. Minimum contingency C20/A40/I40/M20, total 120, primary n=80. Choose target prospectively by documented feasibility/precision rule; n=80 may be inconclusive. One case per independently authored family. I split 50% mapped-null and 50% nontrivial evidence selection, report separately. Original 48 development cases excluded.
- Primary B4-B2 on A+I: case-level binary correctness (3/5 stochastic majority; B2 once plus two-input determinism check), paired Tango score CI in R PropCIs::scoreci.mp; exact McNemar via exact2x2. Primary estimand paired percentage-point difference.
- Key secondary tests Holm-adjusted: B4-B3 case-level mean tokens **all C+A+I+M**; B4-B3 case-level mean end-to-end wall-clock latency **all four**; B4-B6 case-level action agreement **A+I**. For continuous endpoints, 10,000 case-level paired bootstrap resamples, zero differences retained. Prespecify bootstrap CI type, RNG, valid paired p-value procedure for Holm, treatment of failures and missing values before run.
- +/-10pp provisional SESOI ~one additional correct decision per ten uncertain cases; not empirically established practitioner preference. Five exhaustive outcome categories: A d>=10pp and lower CI>0; B 0<d<10pp and lower CI>0; C all otherwise; D d<=-10pp and upper CI<0; E -10pp<d<0 and upper CI<0. A/B indicate positive effect, not proof true effect exceeds 10pp. For B4-B6 use same A-E: only A/B permits a **narrow** superior action-agreement claim; C/D/E prohibits hybrid architectural superiority; even A/B cannot justify whole-architecture superiority without independently measuring cost, safety, reliability. Optional B7 evaluated with same restriction.
- Calculate and archive n=80/120 CI precision simulations, including ~15% discordance; acknowledge n=80 CI ~+/-8–9pp and Outcome C plausible. Confirm exact reviewer wording. Publish immutable public tag or OSF registration **before authoring prospective cases**.
